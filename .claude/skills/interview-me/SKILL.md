name: interview-me
description: Quiz me on the latest merged change as a Customer Engineer interviewer would
disable-model-invocation: true
---
Read the last merged commit and its ADR. Ask me five questions, one at a time,
waiting for my answer before the next: why this design, what breaks at scale,
how I'd debug it live, a security concern, and how I'd explain it to a
non-technical customer. After each answer, say what was strong, what was
vague, and what a strong answer would include. Don't give the answer first.
